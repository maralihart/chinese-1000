01Six rules that always apply

  1. A character is a syllable plus a meaning, not a word. Most words are two characters.
  2. Roughly 80–90% of characters pair a meaning hint with a sound hint. Look for both.
  3. Always learn character + pinyin + tone + meaning as one item, never the shape alone.
  4. Word order does the work English does with endings. Nothing conjugates.
  5. Time goes before the verb. Question words stay where the answer belongs.
  6. 1000 characters ≈ 89% of running text tokens — not 89% comprehension.

Coverage, measured: top 100 characters ≈ 42%, top 500 ≈ 75%, top 1000 ≈ 89%, top 2000 ≈ 97% (Jun Da, Modern Chinese Character Frequency List).

Sandhi: third + third → second + third (你好 is said ní hǎo). 不 becomes bú before a fourth tone. 一 shifts to yì or yí by context. Tone marks sit on the main vowel; write lǜ, not lu.

Sentence patterns & fixes

Say it, ask it, fix it

Ten frames cover most beginner conversation. Swap in any words from page 1.

Ten core sentence patterns with examples
UseStructureExamplePinyin & meaning
Basic statementS + V + O我喝茶。Wǒ hē chá. I drink tea.
IdentityA + 是 + B他是老师。Tā shì lǎoshī. He is a teacher.
DescriptionS + 很 + adj今天很热。Jīntiān hěn rè. Today is hot.
Have / existS + 有 + O我有两个孩子。Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge háizi. I have two children.
LocationS + 在 + place她在学校。Tā zài xuéxiào. She is at school.
Yes/no questionstatement + 吗你是学生吗?Nǐ shì xuésheng ma? Are you a student?
Open questionquestion word in place你吃什么?Nǐ chī shénme? What are you eating?

When it stops working

Shape known, sound lost
Rebuild the card so character, pinyin, tone, and meaning always appear together.
Similar characters blur
Name the differing component out loud: 买 mǎi buy vs 卖 mài sell.
Tones vanish in sentences
Drill two-syllable words, not single characters, and apply the sandhi rules.
Reviews out of control
Cap new items at 5–10 a day and clear the backlog before adding more.
Sentences sound like English
Stop adding words for a week; write five sentences per pattern above.
Sources disagree on levels
Check whether the resource follows HSK 2.0 (6 levels) or HSK 3.0 (9 levels).

Weekly check

  • Cleared every due review
  • Added no more than 50 new items
  • Wrote 10 original sentences
  • Read one graded text aloud
  • Recorded myself once
  • Reviewed tone pairs
  • Checked stroke order for 5 characters
